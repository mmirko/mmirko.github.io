      1 # Support Matrix
      2 
      3 The following tables show the feature state in term of level of development for each instruction and instruction group.
      4 For each of them the support of the features is shown.
      5 
      6 The features are the following:
      7 | Feature | Description |
      8 | --- | --- |
      9 | hdl | The instruction can be translated to hardware description language |
     10 | asm | The instruction can be assembled by the assembler |
     11 | disasm | The instruction can be disassembled by the disassembler |
     12 | hlasm | The instruction can be assembled by the high-level assembler (Basm) |
     13 | asmmeta | The instruction has metadata for the assembler |
     14 | gosim | The instruction can be simulated in the Go-based simulator |
     15 | hdlsim | The instruction can be simulated in the hardware description language simulator |
     16 | mt | Instruction thread support |
     17 
     18 The possible support values are shown below:
     19 
     20 | Value | Meaning |
     21 | --- | --- |
     22 | ![ok](iconok.png) | The feature is fully implemented |
     23 | ![no](iconno.png) | The feature is not yet implemented |
     24 | ![testing](icontesting.png) | The feature is being tested |
     25 | ![partial](iconpartial.png) | The feature is partially implemented |
     26 | ![notapplicable](iconnotapplicable.png) | The feature is not applicable to the instruction |
